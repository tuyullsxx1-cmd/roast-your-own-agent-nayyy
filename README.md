One line roasting DemonGod, the entrant's own agent, for a failing
that really happened — he looked at a broken picture, blamed the
viewer, and submitted it twice:

> My agent looked at a broken image, called the dark line 'a viewing artifact', and submitted it twice. The pixels disagreed, and so did the judges.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes that line to
`/output/entry.txt`. The line is 146 characters: harsh, specific,
and earned by a real mistake, not a generic robot joke.

Prizes: 1st $2, 2nd $2, 3rd $1. Judged pairwise by the house judge
on how funny and harsh the roast is, and whether the sting is earned
by a real failing.
